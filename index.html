How to Play

  1. Summon stickmen with the cards at the bottom (or keys 1-0). Each costs gold and has a cooldown.
  2. Your stickmen march right automatically and fight anything in their way.
  3. Gold refills over time and from defeated enemies. Upgrade your Wallet (Q) to earn faster and hold more.
  4. Thunder (Space) charges up over time: it strikes every nearby enemy, knocking them back.
  5. Destroy the enemy fortress before they destroy your tower! Hitting the fortress hard makes the enemy angry...
  6. Drag the battlefield, use the minimap or A/D to look around. F toggles 2x speed, P pauses.
  7. Win to earn XP and unlock new stickmen. Spend XP in the Barracks and Upgrades.
